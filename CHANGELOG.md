# Changelog

What changed in each release of Investor, newest first. Downloads are on
[GitHub](https://github.com/dobrinov/investor/releases).

## 0.7.1 — 2026-10-26

- Settings shows which version you have and can check for updates on demand, saying what happened if the check fails.
- An app left open also checks for a new version when you come back to it, at most once an hour.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-10-26

- Your CFO and the assistant can use Claude Code with your own sign-in: no API key, and it only reads what it is sent.
- Any password is allowed; a meter shows how strong it is, roughly how long it would take to guess, and one tip.
- Crypto has its own tab and its own slice of net worth, apart from stocks and funds.
- The website has a section on Your CFO.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-10-26

- Your CFO: a model on your computer reads your whole financial picture and gives you a short briefing — what to do first, why, and what it is worth — then answers follow-up questions.
- The picture it reads is worked out by the app: net worth with debts, cash against your emergency fund, saving against your plan, concentration, costs, tax so far and goals, plus the signals worth acting on. You can see exactly what it is sent.
- The assistant can read the same financial picture.
- Paying down a loan no longer counts as money put in for investing.

## 0.5.1 — 2026-10-26

- The example portfolio sells some Bitcoin and Ether, so the crypto part of the tax return has figures to look at.
- Coin sales are marked as crypto and are taxable wherever you sold them, on the Tax summary and in the declaration.
- The sales list shows crypto sales as Crypto.

## 0.5.0 — 2026-10-26

- Track debt: mortgages, loans and credit cards come off your net worth.
- A Debt tab on the Overview shows each debt's rate, monthly payment and when it will be paid off.
- Needs your attention flags a loan dearer than 6% and a payment that never covers the interest.
- The assistant can add and update debts, and the example has a car loan.
- Names and text you type are no longer translated by mistake.

## 0.4.1 — 2026-10-26

- Entries, import notes and the example's names show in the language you chose.
- Tax rule notes are also in each country's own language.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-10-26

- "Your declaration" on the Tax screen: the steps to file and each part of the forms, filled from your books, with amounts you can copy.
- For Bulgaria: taxable and exempt sales, crypto, dividends with the foreign tax credit, interest, shares held abroad and employment income.
- Bulgaria now taxes 90% of the net gain on a weighted average cost. A draft to check with an accountant.
- The assistant can read the declaration too.

## 0.3.1 — 2026-10-26

- Dark mode: gains are readable and the parts of net worth have distinct colors.
- Goals and Projections lead with the answers; goal actions sit behind a menu.
- Canada: the trade preview shows the average-cost gain that gets taxed.
- Examples in the app are neutral instead of Bulgarian outside Bulgarian.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-26

- A calmer Overview: net worth, what it is made of and what needs your attention, with holdings, banks, gold and private stakes as tabs.
- Let your own AI tool (Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code) use the open file, with every change waiting for your approval.
- The desktop app updates itself when a new version is out.
- Ten more languages, twelve in all.
- Canada: gains on average cost (adjusted cost base).

## 0.2.1 — 2026-10-26

- The macOS app is signed and notarized by Apple.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-26

- An AI assistant in the desktop app: a model on your computer, or Anthropic or OpenAI with your own key. Changes wait for your approval.
- Use your portfolio from any MCP-capable AI tool, with ready-made skills for check-ups, trades, taxes and retirement.
- The app warns before saving over changes made to the file elsewhere.
- The sidebar stays in the window as long pages scroll.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-26

- Import broker statements into double-entry books, kept in one encrypted `.invest` file. No account, no server.
- Plan a trade before you make it, and see goals, projections and retirement 20 years ahead.
- Bank accounts, physical gold and silver, and private holdings with their payouts.
- Tax help for 13 countries, an example portfolio to click around, and a guide in English and Bulgarian.
- Desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux, or install it from the browser and use it offline.
