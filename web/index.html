Everything in one place

Import your statements

Drop in a CSV from Interactive Brokers or Revolut. Re-importing the same file adds nothing twice. New brokers are one small config file.

Real books, not a spreadsheet

Every deposit, trade, dividend and fee becomes a balanced double-entry journal entry, with FIFO lots for what each share cost.

Plan a trade first

Before you buy or sell, see the fees, the profit and tax on a sale, how your holdings shift, and the effect on your money in 20 years. Record it only when you are sure.

Goals and projections

Set an emergency fund and savings goals, then see a range of outcomes, not one hopeful line. Try what-if moves and plan your retirement.

Tax help in 11 countries

Realised gains per year with an estimate of the tax, allowances and rate bands for Austria, Bulgaria, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Spain and the UK. The rules are drafts, not tax advice.

Gold coins, bank accounts and private stakes

Physical gold and silver in grams or ounces, savings accounts with no statement to import, and an employer co-investment or private stake: all count in your net worth.

Your data never leaves your computer

No account

Nobody to sign up with. There is no server and no copy of your data anywhere but your own file.

Encrypted file

Your portfolio is one .invest file, encrypted with your password (Argon2id and AES-256-GCM). Keep a backup: a lost password cannot be recovered.

No tracking

No analytics, no cookies, no ads. The app may only talk to the page it was loaded from, so nothing can be sent anywhere else.

Start in a minute

  1. Create a file. Pick a password and save your new .invest file somewhere safe.
  2. Import a statement. Export a CSV from your broker and drop it in.
  3. Plan. Add your goals, try a trade, and come back whenever you invest.
