How it works

Three things it does well

Everything in one encrypted file

Import statements from IBKR and Revolut, add bank accounts, gold and private stakes. It all stays in one .invest file you keep.

Rehearse before you trade

See what a buy or sale does to your cash, your goals and your next 20 years before you record it.

Taxes by country

What to declare, sale by sale, with rules for 13 countries. Draft rules, not tax advice.
